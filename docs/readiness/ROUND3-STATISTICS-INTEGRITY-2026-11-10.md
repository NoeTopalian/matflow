## Not defects (checked)

- **Week comparison:** Reports' "this week vs last week" is already labelled as week so far.
- **Conversion "lost":** it reads the status-event trail, or the current status when there is no trail. Documented.
- **Revenue growth:** it is computed but not shown.

## Business definitions not changed (need Noe)

- **Empty sessions:** Top classes and fill rate count sessions with at least one check-in. Counting empty sessions would lower averages and fill rates.
- **Retention cohort:** the cohort is "joined before the 1st of the club month five months back" (5 to 6 months), while the page says "6+ months".
