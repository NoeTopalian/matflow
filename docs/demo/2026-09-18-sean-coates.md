## The tap path

1. Open the tab → **Scan** (bottom tab).
2. Tap the **12:30** session. **Pick the session before you pick up the stack.**
3. Tap **Start camera** when the stack is in your hand.
4. Hold each card about **20 cm** under the phone. Short buzz = read. A row
   appears with the member's name and *Checked in*. The header counts up.
5. Card three is the revoked one — **described, not demonstrated**. See below.
6. Tap **Open today's register** to show the same names ticked.

## The rules while scanning

- If the phone has locked or the screen dimmed: tap **Stop**, then **Start**.
- **Tapping a different session stops the camera.** Pick the session, then
  Start, then the stack — never change session mid-stack.
- Every card shows *Sending…* the moment it is read and settles within ten
  seconds. *Didn't reach MatFlow* or *MatFlow couldn't record this*: hold the
  card again once; if it fails twice, the register. *Signed out* or *Too many
  at once*: that card goes on the register — rescanning will not retry it.
- If a card says anything other than *Checked in*: say **"I'll take that one on
  the register"** and move on. Do not retry in front of him.
- Do **not** toggle ticks on the register after scanning.
- After the demo, remember last night's rehearsal rows are on yesterday's 18:00
  session if a register or report is opened.

## What he will ask, and the honest answers

**"Does scanning use up one of their classes?"** No — same as the paper
register. Only the kiosk and self check-in draw down a class pack.

**"What if someone loses their card?"** You cancel it from their profile, print
a new one, and the old card is refused the moment it is scanned — it says
*Card cancelled*. **Describe this; do not do it in the room.** Cancelling is
permanent and there is no printer at the gym.

**"Can I scan them all at the end of the session?"** Yes. The scanner does not
care when the class was; scan the stack when the mat clears.

**"What if someone forgot their card?"** Today an owner or manager marks them on
**Mark Attendance** — on the phone that is **More → Mark Attendance**. A coach
cannot from this phone yet.

**"What if the wifi goes?"** There is no offline queue — every scan is live. A
dropped one says *Didn't reach MatFlow* and can be held again once. Switch to
mobile data; failing that, the register by hand.

**"Can two coaches scan at once?"** Yes. Each phone shows its own stack and both
write the same register, which is why the header says *scanned in* and never
claims a class total.

**"How do my members get cards?"** You print them from the members list — a
whole club in one run, two per A4 sheet, or one card from a member's profile
when someone joins. Laminate them yourself or at a print shop; the QR is the
only part that has to survive.

**"What do my members get?"** Their own portal on their phone: their classes,
their belt, their card, their payments. They never see your side.

**"What does it cost?"** There is no fixed public price. Ask what he pays now
and what he is on, then set the fee. Have your number ready before you go in.
